Bug Report: P2090

Active Fault

Fault Sequence

  1. 1. T+0h Car returns from shop. All systems clean.
  2. 2. T+6h False washer fluid warning. Reservoir full.
  3. 3. T+24h Full CEL cascade (P2090). ABS/traction/cruise disabled.

Two Systems, One Fault Point

Body Electronics

  • False washer fluid warning
  • BIU analog input (8.5V ref)
  • NOT on CAN bus

AVCS Fault Accumulation

  • P2090 sets after ~1.5 days
  • EWMA counter converges
  • OCV duty cycle at fault onset

Convergence: Shared electrical infrastructure

The body harness. BIU bridges body CAN and powertrain CAN. Both systems share this wiring path.

Possible Root Causes

High

Bulkhead connector

Known Subaru corrosion point.

High

Body harness degradation

Firewall grommet wear, pin corrosion at ECU connectors B134-B137.

Medium

BIU ground fault

Shared ground instability affects both analog inputs and CAN comms.

Lower

ECU MOSFET degradation

OCV output driver weakening over time. Lower probability given body-side symptom.

Engine harness was replaced in 2022 and did not fix P2090.

Proposed Testing Procedure

Next Step
  1. 1 Clear DTCs via ECUTek. Resets fault counters.
  2. 2

    Add these 6 parameters to the logging profile

    Battery Voltage (V)

    Voltage dips correlating with AVCS deviation confirm electrical fault

    Exhaust VVT Retard Angle Right (°)

    Both banks needed to confirm fault is left-side only

    OCV Duty Exhaust Left (%)

    Erratic or 0% = circuit fault

    OCV Duty Exhaust Right (%)

    L/R asymmetry confirms isolated fault

    AVCS Exhaust Left Target (°)

    Target dropping to 0 degrees = ECU giving up

    Oil Temperature (°F)

    AVCS needs warm oil, rules out hydraulic cause

    21 current params + 6 = 27 total. Still maintains 10+ Hz sample rate.

  3. 3 Datalog every drive until the CEL returns.
  4. 4 Upload datafile for analysis.

This Helps Diagnose

  • Voltage dips correlating with AVCS deviation across drives = electrical fault, not mechanical
  • Narrows the fix to bulkhead connector or body harness replacement
  • Takes engine-side components off the table permanently
